# NeuroWeb & bridging TRAC

NeuroWeb is one of the chains DKG V10 supports (alongside Base and Gnosis). It is a decentralized AI blockchain designed to incentivize knowledge creation, connectivity, and sharing through **knowledge mining**. Its native token, **NEURO**, fuels the AI knowledge economy by rewarding contributions to the OriginTrail DKG.

NeuroWeb is a permissionless, EVM-enabled blockchain secured by Polkadot validators. It evolved from the original OriginTrail Parachain via a community governance vote in December 2023. Dedicated documentation lives at [docs.neuroweb.ai](https://docs.neuroweb.ai/).

## NEURO token

NEURO is the native token of NeuroWeb, used to pay gas and to incentivize knowledge mining. More information is in the [official NeuroWeb documentation](https://docs.neuroweb.ai/neuro-token).

## Bridging TRAC to NeuroWeb

To use TRAC on NeuroWeb (for funding nodes, staking, or publishing), you need to bridge TRAC to NeuroWeb.

You can transfer TRAC between Ethereum and NeuroWeb via [Snowbridge](https://app.snowbridge.network/). Step-by-step bridging instructions are in the [NeuroWeb documentation](https://docs.neuroweb.ai/ethereum-neuroweb-trac-bridge).

## Adding TRAC on NeuroWeb to your wallet

The TRAC token address is the same on both NeuroWeb mainnet and testnet:

```
0xFfFFFFff00000000000000000000000000000001
```

To add it in MetaMask (connected to NeuroWeb): open the **Assets** tab → **Import tokens** → paste the TRAC token address above (the remaining fields auto-populate) → **Add custom token**. Your TRAC balance will then display in MetaMask.
